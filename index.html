A LITTLE ABOUT ME

People first.
Security always.

I’m Tamara, a cybersecurity strategist, educator, and speaker. My work spans Splunk SURGe and Cisco Talos, but my purpose is simple: help people understand security and stay safe.

Security knowledge should be accessible whether you work in the industry, are finding your way into it, or have never opened a security dashboard. I create content for curious people at every level, from first questions to leadership decisions.

My background in criminal justice and anthropology keeps people at the center of how I explain technical topics. Away from security, I’m usually golfing, trying a new recipe, playing video games, or keeping up with my boxers.
